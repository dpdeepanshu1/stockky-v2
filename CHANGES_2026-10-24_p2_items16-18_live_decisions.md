## Item 17 - short-side shortlist, LIVE
`screening/shortlist.py::build_short` ranks the bearish short candidates (score 0-100): scan share 0.35, Market Board **bearish** conviction 0.35, zone score 0.15, weak chart (100 - technical score) 0.15, minus `SHORT_SHORTLIST_EXTENDED_PENALTY` (15) when the board marks it extended below its zone. Parts that have no data are left out and the weights renormalised; ties keep scan order. `run_short_cycle` now quality-checks up to `SHORT_SHORTLIST_MAX` names (was `QUALITY_GATE_TOP_N`, 3), ranks them and walks them **best first**, in shadow AND live mode, so the capital and the `SHORT_MAX_POSITIONS` slots go to the strongest short. It never adds a candidate and never loosens a gate: every name still walks the same ten fail-closed gates. Any error falls back to scan order. The latest ranking (score, parts, outcome ENTERED / REFUSED) is on `GET /short/shadow` under `shortlist`.
Switches (live, protected): `SHORT_SHORTLIST_ENABLED` (true; false = scan order), `SHORT_SHORTLIST_MAX` (5, 1..10).

## Item 18 - short bar replay, LIVE as a runner job
`scripts/short_history_replay.py` (stdlib) + runner job `short_history_replay` (weekly group, Friday plan row, position-stocks tab). Pretends to SELL at fixed times of day on recent 5-minute bars of 20 liquid NSE names (from market-data `/history`), walks stop and target (a bar touching both = stop; target booked at its level), covers what is still open at 14:55, charges flat 0.08% round trip, and runs the same for BUY as a control. Break-even aware (at 2:1 a short needs to win 1 in 3). Verdict `SHORT REPLAY: PASS | FAIL - reason | NOT_ENOUGH_DATA - progress`; a FAIL says when the long control earned (a rising window, weak evidence against the short rule). Exit 1 only on FAIL. It gates nothing: the live gate is the real short shadow / live record.
**Not verified here:** how far back market-data serves 5-minute bars. If it serves too little the job says NOT_ENOUGH_DATA and names the symbols with no bars.

## Item 16 leftovers - decided
- **VIX caution at its own percentile - DONE, live.** New `VIX_CAUTION_PCTILE` (75; 0 = off). `screening/vix_gate.py` fetches ~12 months of India VIX daily closes once a day (market-data `/history/INDIAVIX`, failure retried after 10 min) and the size shrink also starts at that percentile. **The LOWER of the fixed `VIX_CAUTION_ABOVE` and the percentile applies, so it can only add caution.** No history = fixed level only (fail open). Why: a fixed 18 means different things in a calm year and a wild one; ranking VIX against its own year is the standard way to read it.
- **Pre-open gap as a multiple of the stock's own ATR - DONE, live.** New `PREOPEN_GAP_ATR_MULT` (1.5; 0 = off) and `PREOPEN_GAP_ATR_FLOOR_PCT` (1.5, config only). `screening/preopen.py::atr_gap_threshold` = mult x the stock's daily ATR % (never under the floor); the LOWER of that and the fixed 6% / 3% blocks, so it can only block more. A calm stock's 2% gap now counts; a wild stock's does not. No ATR data = fixed % only. **This changes live behaviour at the shipped value** (more gap blocks on calm stocks, until 10:00); way back `PREOPEN_GAP_ATR_MULT=0`.
- **`WINDOW_AUTODISABLE_MIN_TRADES` 8 -> 30 - DECIDED: keep 8.** At 8 trades a win rate is very noisy, but the two mistakes are not equal: disabling a good window too early costs missed trades, disabling a bad one too late costs real money, and the record is about 196 trades at about -3,134 rupees. Loosening a safety on a losing record is the wrong direction. Nothing changed.
