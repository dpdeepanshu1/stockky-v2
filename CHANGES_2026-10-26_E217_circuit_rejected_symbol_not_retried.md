# E217 - real-trade entry: a symbol Dhan rejected as outside the price band is not bought again the same day

**Why:** `entry_engine/entry.py::evaluate_mode` reads the Dhan rejection "outside the exchange price band" and logs "Skipping this candidate for the rest of today", but nothing remembered it. The next candidate row for the same symbol went through every gate, was sized, sent to Dhan and rejected again, each time spending the shared Dhan order budget (shared with position-stocks) and writing another REJECTED order and a "BUY rejected" Telegram alert.
**Change:** `_CIRCUIT_REJECTED` (process memory, `{(mode, symbol): IST date}`, same style as `_UNCERTAIN_BUYS`) is set in the circuit-limit reject branch. A new Gate 1c, right after the actionable-label gate and before the price gate, turns such a candidate into a WAIT (`gate_tag = "circuit_rejected_today"`, no order, no broker call). Another symbol, another mode and the next IST day are unaffected. A restart forgets it, and Dhan then simply rejects once more.
**Not changed:** the gates, sizing, risk engine, exits and the pre-trade circuit gate (group 329) are untouched. No config switch: the block only repeats what the existing log line already promised.
**Review (no other change):** real-trade `entry evaluate_mode` is now read line by line (gates 1 to 6, staging, ranking, placement, invalid-IP early return, end of cycle). Nothing else needed a fix. Your call, not changed: the staged symbol's reserved cash is not released for candidates Gate 6 drops (documented in the code as deliberate and harmless once the loop ends).
**Tests:** `python3 -m py_compile` on entry.py and the new test; the two helpers were exercised in isolation (mark, same-day hit, other symbol, other mode, old date). pytest NOT run (not installed here): `pytest tests/test_e217_circuit_rejected_symbol_not_retried.py` in real-trade-service.
