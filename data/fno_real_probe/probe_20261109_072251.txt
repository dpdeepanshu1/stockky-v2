WHAT THIS DOES: 1) pick the contract and read its live bid / ask  2) BUY 1 lot with a LIMIT order at the ask  3) read the fill back from Dhan  4) hold a few seconds
                5) SELL it with a LIMIT order at the bid  6) work out profit / loss and the charges and show where every rupee went.
It does NOT use the engine's locks, arm or database. Orders are INTRADAY product: nothing can be carried overnight.
