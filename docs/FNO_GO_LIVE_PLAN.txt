### Round 293 (10 Nov 2026): the entry BUY with a failed call
* **Gap closed:** an entry BUY whose POST failed with a network error or HTTP 5xx made no position row, although the LIMIT order could be resting at Dhan and fill later: a long with no stop and no row of ours, and the next cycle could BUY again. Round 292 had closed the same hole for the stop and the exit SELL, not for the entry.
* **Fix (`fno/engine.py`):** the BUY carries our own correlationId (`bu...`). On a maybe-sent failure the engine asks Dhan (`GET /orders/external/{id}`, read only). Found: handled as placed (settle: wait, cancel what rests, read the fill, stop for exactly the filled lots). Rejected / cancelled with nothing filled: no row. Cannot tell (or 404 inside 2 minutes): kept as a flagged `entry_state=UNKNOWN` row with `entry_cid`: no stop, never sold, blocks a second BUY; `resolve_unknown_entries` asks again every cycle (404 after 2 minutes = never arrived, closed NOT_FILLED; found resting = cancelled, what filled decides).
* **Older gap closed on the way:** a part-filled entry flagged UNKNOWN (cancel not confirmed) now settles once Dhan shows the cancel: the filled whole lots are kept with a stop (before it stayed flagged, with no stop, forever). Less than one lot filled stays flagged and loud.
* **Frontend:** amber-red "ENTRY UNKNOWN" chip and message on the position card, "N entry unknown" chip on the Overview header. Read-only: it places and clears nothing.
* **No Calibration switch on purpose:** it is a safety rule for real money, a switch to turn it off would only re-open the hole.
* **Tests:** `tests/test_round293_entry_lookup.py` (15; 13 fail on the round 292 engine, 2 pin unchanged behaviour); `fnoView.test.mjs` round 293 block. Two older tests that asserted "no row is kept after a failed BUY" (`test_round277_network`, `test_round282_exit_doubt`) now assert the flagged row.
* **VM:** `scripts/fno_live_check.py` is a read-only look at the engine during market hours (see the round 293 message for the command).
* **Still unverified live:** Dhan's reply for an unknown correlationId (only a plain 404 is read as "not there").

## 3. Go-live plan with exit criteria

Nothing here proves a profit; each phase only decides whether the next one is allowed.

**Phase 0 - verify the tooling (this week, needs the VM).**
- Full F&O suite on the VM (`python3 -m pytest -q`).
- One-week fetch of ATM, ATM+-1, ATM+-2 and `fno_rolling_check.py`: settles bar-start timestamps, `spot` being the index and the ATM label following spot (the PUT file in the stand-in test shows what a shifted file looks like).
- Expiry-day behaviour of `expiryCode 1` (3 Nov snippet).
- Confirm SENSEX id 51 in the scrip master before any SENSEX fetch.
- Exit: suite green, checker clean, no 429 (raise `--pause` otherwise).

**Phase 1 - the rolling backtest (about 1 week).**
- Fetch about six months (`--resume` now also extends a range), export index daily and 5-minute CSVs, and a **daily India VIX file (required since Round 260)**.
- Run `fno_rolling_backtest.py` per strategy.
- Exit per strategy: `data_quality.ok` true, at least 30 trades, positive net after charges, the folds not all depending on one stretch. A strategy that is `NOT_PROVEN` stays OFF.

**Phase 2 - DEMO evidence (at least 4 weeks and 60 trades).**
- `FNO_ENGINE_ENABLED` ON, mode DEMO, `FNO_RECORD_SNAPSHOTS` ON; only CORE plus the strategies that passed Phase 1.
- Daily after 15:30: decision log and trades. Weekly: replay backtest on the recorded days, compared with DEMO and with Phase 1.
- Exit: replay and DEMO agree, net positive after charges, drawdown you can live with, no unexplained skipped stop. Expiry-day strategies need 30+ DEMO expiry days (about 7 months) and stay DEMO until then.
- Earliest date: if DEMO starts about 11 Nov, 4 weeks puts the first possible REAL decision around 9 Dec; with the 3-trades-a-day cap, 60 trades may take longer.

**Phase 3 - first REAL week.**
- Before: Dhan static-IP whitelist for API orders, one manual trade compared with History > Charges, `CAPITAL_ALLOC_MODE` shadow for about a week with a fresh board, reconcile check clean.
- Settings: `FNO_MAX_LOTS` 1, `FNO_REAL_ENABLED` ON, mode REAL, `Arm today` by hand every morning (it expires at midnight IST), reconcile after the close, never on an expiry day.
- Stop at once (disarm, engine OFF, square off all) on any reconcile mismatch, any order that did not do what the log says, or the daily loss limit.

**Phase 4 - scale slowly.** One change at a time, a week apart; never raise lots or risk % to recover a loss. Thresholds are calibrated from the results, not from one day.
